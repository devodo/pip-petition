About PIP

PIP (Plastic Is Pollution) is a petition started by a group of friends campaigning for the government to treat plastic pollution as the environmental emergency it is.

Our story

PIP was started by our friend Lila. On a trip to Cyprus, she was struck by the amount of litter she saw — plastic scattered along beaches, roadsides and beauty spots that should have been pristine. She came home convinced that this wasn't just a local litter problem, but a symptom of something much bigger: the world's attitude to pollution needs to change.

So she started this petition, and we — her friends — joined her. We're not a big organisation, just a group of friends who believe that if enough of us speak up, the people with the power to act will have to listen.

What we're asking for

1. Recognition

Officially classify plastic waste as pollution, so it is monitored, reported and regulated like other pollutants.

2. Reduction targets

Set legally binding targets to cut single-use plastic production and consumption, with clear deadlines.

3. Accountability

Make the largest plastic producers responsible for the full lifecycle of their packaging — not taxpayers and volunteers.

4. Investment

Fund proper recycling infrastructure and support research into genuinely sustainable alternatives to plastic.

Why now?

Plastic doesn't go away. Every piece of plastic ever produced still exists in some form, and microplastics have now been found in our food, our water and even our bodies. The longer we wait, the bigger the clean-up becomes — and some of the damage can never be undone.

Governments respond when enough people speak up. That's where you come in.
