What We're Doing

The petition is just one part of the campaign. Here's what we're working on right now, what's coming next, and what we've already done.

Happening now

Happening now

Collecting signatures

Our main focus is growing the petition. We're sharing it with our friends, families, classmates and online — every signature makes our voice louder.

Happening now

Awareness campaign

We're making posters and sharing posts explaining why plastic is pollution — not just litter — and what everyone can do about it.

Happening now

Picking up litter

We pick up litter when we're out and about, and we keep track of how much plastic we find — it all becomes evidence for the petition.

Coming up

Coming up

Presenting the petition

Once we reach our signature target, we'll formally deliver the petition document to our local MP and ask them to raise it in Parliament.

Coming up

Fundraising for a community litter pick

We're planning ways to save up money so we can run a community litter-picking event — with proper litter pickers, gloves and bags so everyone who turns up can join in.

Completed

Completed

Launched the petition

We launched PIP and this website to start collecting signatures and spreading the word.

How you can help

Sign the petition, share it with friends and family, and join us at a clean-up. If you'd like to help out, get in touch — we'd love to have you.
